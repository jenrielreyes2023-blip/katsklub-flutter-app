========================================================================
KATSKLUB FLUTTER - SUNRISE POSTCARD GRADIENT & BANNER (HANDOFF DOCUMENT)
========================================================================

Ito ang detalye kung paano natin na-achieve at naperfect ang Sunrise Postcard Theme para sa user na si "daisy".

1. BORDERLESS HORIZONTAL GRAPHIC GENERATION
   - Kumuha tayo ng isang dynamic vector design ng sunrise (sun, clouds, at flying birds) na wide horizontal panoramic layout.
   - Ginamitan natin ito ng ImageMagick at transparent fuzz filtering para matanggal ang lahat ng black backgrounds at JPEG compression noise nang buo. Ang natira na lamang ay ang malinis at transparent na sun, white/peach clouds, at flying birds na walang kahit anong dark border o outline.
   - Inilagay ang final asset sa: assets/images/sunrise_sticker.png

2. FULL-WIDTH DISPLAY CONFIGURATION
   - Sa halip na ilagay ito sa isang gilid (kung saan mukha siyang maliit o nakapatong lang), binago natin ang layout logic sa parehong screen widgets:
     * lib/widgets/post_card.dart
     * lib/screens/post_detail_screen.dart
   - Pinalitan ang Positioned coordinates mula right-aligned papunta sa full stretch:
     * left: 0 at right: 0 (sasakupin ang buong lapad ng post header).
     * top: 0.0 (lalapat mismo sa tuktok ng card).
     * fit: BoxFit.cover upang maging flexible at responsive ang pag-scale sa kahit anong screen size habang maganda ang pagkaka-center.

3. CODE-LEVEL SHADERMASK FADE (THE PERFECT BLENDING SECRET)
   - Dahil gumagamit ang image ng BoxFit.cover, pinuputol ng Flutter ang gilid at ilalim ng image para magkasya sa Aspect Ratio ng card. Kinakain nito ang kahit anong opacity fade na nilagay natin direct sa PNG image file.
   - Upang masolusyunan ito, binalot natin ang Image widget sa isang 'ShaderMask'.
   - Ang ShaderMask ay may LinearGradient na nagsisimula sa white (opaque) sa taas at nagtatapos sa transparent sa ilalim:
     * 0.0 to 0.7 height = Fully Opaque (para malinaw ang sun, birds, at top clouds).
     * 0.7 to 1.0 height = Smooth Fade to Transparent (para sa ilalim na bahagi).
   - Sa pamamagitan nito, ang ilalim ng pink clouds ay dahan-dahans at swabeng nagfe-fade out at sumasama sa natural na background gradient at white body ng postcard, kaya walang "cut" o matigas na linya na makikita sa visual.

Ang resulta ay isang premium at eleganteng layout kung saan ang sunrise decoration ay swabe at mukhang natural na parte ng background.

KATSKLUB FLUTTER - OCEAN POSTCARD THEME FINAL DIRECTION (HANDOFF DOCUMENT)
========================================================================

Ito ang final approved direction para sa Ocean Postcard Theme. Kung may susunod na AI na gagalaw dito, ito ang baseline na dapat sundan at huwag nang bumalik sa old full-width dark-ocean approach.

1. VISUAL DIRECTION (IMPORTANT)
   - Hindi dapat punuin ng ocean graphics ang buong top area nang pantay-pantay dahil natatamaan at nahihirapang basahin ang author name at timestamp.
   - Ang tamang final direction ay:
     * LEFT SIDE = very light, airy, quiet reading zone
     * RIGHT SIDE = stronger teal/blue decorative weight
   - Ibig sabihin, ang left 55% to 60% ng banner ay dapat sobrang light aqua / near-white / misty seafoam para safe ang metadata readability.
   - Ang right side lang ang may mas saturated ocean blues at decorative coral cluster.

2. COMPOSITION RULES
   - Wide horizontal panoramic banner pa rin ang theme asset.
   - Sa left side:
     * Minimal lang ang detail
     * Very faint tiny bubbles lang
     * Walang coral na dapat tumama sa author info area
   - Sa right side:
     * Dalawang coral pieces lang dapat
     * One medium coral + one small coral
     * Diyan naka-concentrate ang richer teal / ocean-blue / aqua highlights
   - Goal nito ay premium postcard/banner feel, hindi sticker dump at hindi busy wallpaper.

3. FINAL APPROVED OCEAN ASSET
   - Current approved latest asset used by the app:
     * assets/images/ocean_sticker_v3.png
   - Huwag nang gumamit ng old versions as the main active asset unless kailangan talaga ng rollback:
     * assets/images/ocean_sticker.png
     * assets/images/ocean_sticker_v2.png
   - Ang v3 ang may tamang improved direction:
     * left side lighter and cleaner
     * right side stronger color mass
     * coral cluster controlled and minimal

4. APP WIRED FILES USING THE LATEST OCEAN ASSET
   - Ito ang current active references ng Ocean Theme:
     * lib/screens/shop_screen.dart
     * lib/widgets/post_card.dart
     * lib/screens/post_detail_screen.dart
   - Ang expected referenced asset ngayon sa code ay:
     * assets/images/ocean_sticker_v3.png

5. IMPORTANT UI PRINCIPLE FOR FUTURE CHANGES
   - Kapag may magpapabago ulit ng Ocean Theme, preserve this principle:
     * readability first
     * decorative intensity second
   - Huwag ibalik sa dark/full decorative coverage ang left side.
   - Huwag paramihin ang corals, shells, fish, or heavy bubbles.
   - Keep it clean, premium, and biased to the right side only.

6. IF A FUTURE AI NEEDS TO REGENERATE AGAIN
   - The correct prompt direction should stay close to this:
     * very light clean left reading zone
     * smooth fade into richer teal-blue right side
     * strongest color concentrated around the two coral pieces only
     * faint bubbles only on the left
     * no clutter
     * no central subject
     * no heavy decoration behind metadata area

Ang final intention ng Ocean Theme ay parang elegant postcard header na may clean readable left zone at tasteful ocean accent sa right, hindi full decorative wallpaper sa buong top area.

KATSKLUB FLUTTER - GEMINI ROGER THEMES CROPPING DIRECTION (HANDOFF DOCUMENT)
========================================================================

Ito ang critical direction para sa Gemini-exclusive Roger postcard themes. Kung gagalawin ulit ito ng susunod na AI, huwag nang ulitin ang mistake na ginawang sobrang liit o buong asset ang pinakita.

1. VISUAL GOAL
   - Ang tamang look para sa Gemini Roger themes ay cinematic crop, hindi full-asset showcase.
   - Dapat impactful pa rin ang banner, pero hindi rin dapat puro ulo lang ang makita.
   - Ibig sabihin:
     * huwag gumamit ng BoxFit.contain para sa regular posts
     * huwag biglang ipakita ang buong character dahil nawawala ang postcard/banner feel
     * small crop/alignment/height adjustments lang ang dapat gawin

2. CURRENT APPROVED DIRECTION
   - Active Gemini assets:
     * assets/images/gemini_roger_hunter_v1.png
     * assets/images/gemini_roger_wolf_v1.png
   - Current render tuning in code:
     * lib/widgets/post_card.dart
     * lib/screens/post_detail_screen.dart
   - Important current values:
     * Hunter regular-post height = 78
     * Wolf regular-post height = 76
     * Hunter discussion height = 128
     * Wolf discussion height = 124
   - Current approved crop style:
     * BoxFit.cover pa rin
     * Hunter alignment = Alignment(0.4, -0.2)
     * Wolf alignment = Alignment(0.7, -0.05)

3. WHAT TO AVOID
   - Huwag gawing BoxFit.contain ang Hunter or Wolf sa regular posts.
   - Huwag ibaba nang todo ang height na parang thumbnail na lang sila.
   - Huwag rin taasan nang sobra ang crop na nagiging ulo-only framing.
   - Kapag may complaint sa framing, micro-tweak lang:
     * 2 to 4 px sa height
     * maliit na alignment shift lang
   - Huwag gumawa ng drastic reset.

4. PRACTICAL RULE
   - If the user says the previous version looked better and only needed to be lowered a bit, believe that literally:
     * keep the same cinematic crop feel
     * lower or relax the framing slightly
     * do not switch to a full visible asset approach

KATSKLUB BACKEND - POST DETAIL POSTCARD THEME DEPENDENCY (HANDOFF DOCUMENT)
========================================================================

May important backend dependency ang postcard themes na madaling makalimutan kapag tama sa feed pero mali sa post page.

1. ROOT CAUSE WE ALREADY HIT
   - Feed cards puwedeng tama ang postcard theme dahil galing sila sa payload na may author_postcard_theme.
   - Pero ang post detail page gumagamit ng fresh GET /api/posts/:postId call.
   - Noong una, plain ang post page dahil iyong route na iyon kulang ang author_postcard_theme sa returned payload.

2. REQUIRED BACKEND FIELD
   - Sa /home/ubuntu/katsklub/server.js, ang GET /api/posts/:postId query must include:
     * COALESCE(u.profile_border, '') AS author_profile_border
     * COALESCE(u.postcard_theme, '') AS author_postcard_theme

3. REQUIRED FRONTEND EXPECTATION
   - Ang Flutter Post model already supports:
     * authorProfileBorder
     * authorPostcardTheme
   - Ang PostDetailScreen loads an initial post, then replaces it with loadPost(widget.postId).
   - Kaya kung kulang ang field sa detail endpoint, mawawala ang theme sa post page kahit tama sa feed.

4. IF THIS REGRESSION HAPPENS AGAIN
   - Kapag gumagana ang postcard theme sa feed pero plain sa post page:
     * check GET /api/posts/:postId response first
     * verify author_postcard_theme exists in the payload
     * restart PM2 app `katsklub` if the route code was updated but not yet live
